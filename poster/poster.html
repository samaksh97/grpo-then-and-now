An Interactive Guide to Modern Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning

One ideaGRPO learns only when its samples contain informative contrasts, and fails when the group or the verifier provides none.
Why it mattersUsed in training DeepSeek-V3, DeepSeek-R1 and Qwen3 · built into TRL, verl and OpenRLHF · revised by three NeurIPS 2025 papers

1Anatomy of a GRPO update: every highlighted choice is a failure mode

Sample G answers per prompt, score each with a verifier, compare each answer only with its own group: no critic. ri,t = πθ/πold per token; the KL term keeps the policy near a reference.

2Trigger it, then fix it

1Zero-gradient groups

If all G rewards agree, std = 0 and every advantage is 0: the group teaches nothing. It gets worse as the model improves.

{{CHART:no_gradient}}
DAPO · Dynamic SamplingDrop all-equal groups and refill the batch, at the cost of extra generation.

2Rare tokens can't grow

The clip bounds the ratio, not the probability, so rare correct tokens barely move and exploration collapses.

{{CHART:clip}}
DAPO · Clip-Higher | DisCORaise only the upper bound to 0.28 (DAPO), or drop the clip for a KL constraint (DisCO).

3Length bias

Averaging per answer first: short answers weigh more per token, and long wrong answers are penalized less per token.

{{CHART:length}}
DAPO · Token-level lossAverage over every token in the batch; Dr. GRPO uses a constant divisor.

4Truncation noise

A hard cut at Lmax scores a long but correct answer as wrong, with no warning before the wall.

{{CHART:overlong}}
DAPO · Overlong filtering and shapingMask truncated answers in the loss, and add a graded penalty before the limit.

5Difficulty bias

Dividing by the group's std weights each question by √(p(1−p)): nearly solved and nearly hopeless questions fade out.

{{CHART:difficulty}}
DisCO · Discriminative objectiveScore answers discriminatively with no difficulty weight: every question counts the same.

6Overthinking

An outcome reward pays the same for a correct answer after 200 tokens or 2,000, so redundant reasoning is free.

{{CHART:serial_group}}
S-GRPO · Serial exitsCut one chain at several points: earlier correct exits earn more, wrong ones earn 0.
